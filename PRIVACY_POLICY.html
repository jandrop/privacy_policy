Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed by Jandrop (the “Developer”). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This document explains what information is handled by our apps and how it is protected.

1. We Do Not Collect Personal Data

Our applications are designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or device data to our servers or third parties. All processing happens locally on your device.

2. Information Entered by You

Some apps may require certain information to function (such as server addresses, tokens, or configuration details). This information is:

  • Entered manually by you.
  • Stored securely on your device only.
  • Never transmitted to the Developer or third-party servers.

We do not have access to this information under any circumstance.

3. Information Accessed by the App

Depending on the app’s functionality, it may read data from local services or devices you connect to (such as system metrics, server status, or device information).

This data is used solely to provide the functionality of the app and is not stored permanently or transmitted anywhere.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscription Management

Some apps offer optional paid features or subscriptions processed through the platform store (Google Play or App Store). To manage subscriptions, validate purchases, and restore entitlements, we use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), a third-party service.

RevenueCat may collect and process the following data on our behalf:

  • Anonymous device identifiers (e.g. RevenueCat App User ID).
  • Purchase receipts and subscription status provided by the platform store.
  • App version and platform (iOS / Android).

This data is used exclusively to verify and manage your subscription entitlements. No personally identifiable information (name, email, payment details) is shared with RevenueCat. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Push Notifications (Optional Feature)

Some apps offer optional push notifications. This feature is entirely opt-in and can be enabled or disabled at any time from within the app's settings. When you enable push notifications, the following third-party services are involved:

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a service provided by Google LLC, to deliver push notifications to your device. When you enable this feature, Firebase generates a device registration token — a pseudonymous identifier assigned to your device. This token does not contain your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cloudflare (Notification Relay)

To route notifications from your Unraid server to your device, the FCM token is sent to a relay service hosted on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.). This service stores the token solely to forward notifications triggered by your own server. No other data is associated with or transmitted alongside the token. When you disable push notifications, the token is deleted from this service.

You can review Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

6. No Other External Communication

Beyond the services described in sections 4 and 5, our apps:

  • Do not upload personal data to remote servers.
  • Do not use analytics services.
  • Do not include tracking technologies.
  • Do not integrate with advertising networks.

7. Data Security

Any sensitive information stored locally—such as tokens or passwords—is protected using secure storage mechanisms provided by the device’s operating system.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our applications are not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect improvements or new app features. Updates will be posted on this page and become effective immediately.
