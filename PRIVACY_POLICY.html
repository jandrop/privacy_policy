Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to U-Manager — Client for Unraid, developed by Jandrop (the "Developer"). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This document explains what information is handled by the app and how it is protected.

1. We Do Not Collect Personal Data

U-Manager is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or device data to our servers or third parties, except as described in sections 4 and 5 below. All other processing happens locally on your device.

2. Information Entered by You

U-Manager requires certain information to function (such as server addresses, tokens, or configuration details). This information is:

  • Entered manually by you.
  • Stored securely on your device only.
  • Never transmitted to the Developer or third-party servers.

We do not have access to this information under any circumstance.

3. Information Accessed by the App

U-Manager reads data from the Unraid server you connect to (such as system metrics, server status, or device information). This data is used solely to provide the functionality of the app and is not stored permanently or transmitted anywhere else.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscription Management

U-Manager offers optional paid features or subscriptions. Purchases are processed by the platform store (Google Play or App Store) — we never see your payment details.

Like any app with in-app purchases, an anonymous identifier is stored to remember what you have bought, so you can restore your purchases at any time without paying again. This is the same mechanism used by every app on the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage this, which receives only an anonymous ID, your purchase receipt, and the app version — nothing that identifies you personally. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Push Notifications (Optional Feature)

U-Manager offers optional push notifications to alert you about events on your Unraid server. This feature is entirely opt-in and can be enabled or disabled at any time from the app's settings.

Push notifications work the same way in every app on your phone: the operating system assigns your device a delivery token — a random code with no connection to your identity — and that token is used to route the notification to the right device. Without it, there is no way for any app to deliver push notifications.

This token is stored solely to forward notifications triggered by your own Unraid server to your device. No other data is transmitted. When you disable push notifications, the token is deleted.

The delivery infrastructure uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and Cloudflare Workers. You can review their privacy policies at policies.google.com/privacy and cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

6. No Other External Communication

Beyond the services described in sections 4 and 5, U-Manager:

  • Does not upload personal data to remote servers.
  • Does not use analytics services.
  • Does not include tracking technologies.
  • Does not integrate with advertising networks.

7. Data Security

Any sensitive information stored locally — such as tokens or passwords — is protected using secure storage mechanisms provided by the device's operating system.

8. Children's Privacy

U-Manager is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect improvements or new app features. Updates will be posted on this page and become effective immediately.
